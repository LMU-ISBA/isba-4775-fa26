# Register your domain

Before class on Thursday, October 1, 2026

On Thursday you'll start pointing a domain at your site, so register one
before class. Both options below use Namecheap and take about ten minutes.

Pick a name you'd be glad to hand an employer, such as your first and last
name. The domain stays yours after the course, and it's going on your resume.

## Option 1: a free .me through the Student Pack

This one needs an approved GitHub Student Developer Pack. You redeem it
through Namecheap's student site, nc.me, not through the GitHub page.

1. Go to https://nc.me/landing/github, or sign in directly at
   https://nc.me/github/login.
2. Authorize with GitHub. That's how Namecheap confirms your Pack.
3. Search for a name with the `.me` domain extension and add it to the cart.
4. Click "Complete Order," then sign in to Namecheap or create an account.
   Use your own account, since you'll own the domain.
5. Finish checkout. With only the free .me in the cart, it should cost $0 and
   ask for no card.
6. The domain shows up in your Namecheap dashboard within a few minutes.

The first year is free. After that, .me renews at $23.98 a year.

## Option 2: a .com for $6.79

If you don't want .me, or your Pack isn't approved yet, new Namecheap
customers can get a .com for $6.79 for the first year.

1. Search for your name at https://www.namecheap.com and add the .com to the
   cart.
2. At checkout, enter the promo code `NEWCOM679`.
3. Confirm the first year shows $6.79, plus a $0.20 ICANN fee, before you pay.

The code is for new customers only, and it's limited to one domain per person
or household. After the first year, .com renews at $18.48 a year. If you
already have a Namecheap account, the Pack price for a .com through nc.me is
$8.88.

Over two years, the two options cost about the same. The .me is free now and
renews higher, and the .com costs a little now and renews lower.

## Other domain extensions

You're welcome to pick a different domain extension, such as .dev or .io, if
you'd rather pay for it. Check the renewal price before you buy, not just the
first-year price. Some TLDs cost under a dollar the first year and much more
after that.

## At checkout

- Domain privacy is free at Namecheap, so make sure it's on. It keeps your
  name and home address out of the public records for the domain.
- Skip the add-ons, such as hosting, email, SSL, and premium DNS. Our site
  doesn't need them.
- Decide about auto-renew now. If you turn it off, put the expiration date in
  your calendar, because an expired domain takes your site down.

## After you buy

Don't change the domain's DNS settings or name servers yet. We'll move DNS to
Cloudflare together in class on Thursday. Create your free Cloudflare account
at https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up before then.

Write down what you paid, the renewal date, and the renewal price. They go in
your Project 1 cost inventory.

If your Pack isn't approved, the household limit blocks you, or checkout
asks for something unexpected, send me a Teams message before Thursday.
