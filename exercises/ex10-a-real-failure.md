# Exercise 10: One real failure

Due Tuesday, December 8, 2026, at 1:45 PM. 15 points. Submit your Project 02
repository URL and the path to `docs/exercise-10.md` in Brightspace.

Things have broken while you built Project 02. A deploy failed, a page
showed a 502, SSH timed out, or your agent gave a wrong answer. Choose one
failure that happened to you and write down how you worked through it. Your
final interview asks you to diagnose a failure, and this is the practice
for it.

## Choose the failure

It has to come from your own Project 02 work. Nothing is staged for this
exercise. If you can't remember one, ask your coding agent in your Project
02 repository:

```text
Look through this repository's history, my notes, and our past
sessions. List the failures I worked through in this project, with
the date and what broke. Don't write them up. I'll choose one.
```

## Write it in your own words

In `docs/exercise-10.md`, answer five questions, in a few sentences each:

1. What did you see? Give the error or the wrong result, and where you saw
   it.
2. What did you think the cause was at first?
3. What did you check, and what did each check tell you?
4. What was the cause?
5. What fixed it, and how do you know it's fixed?

Your agent can remind you what happened. The writing has to be yours,
because you'll tell this story in the interview without notes.

Keep passwords and keys out of the file. Then commit and push.

## How credit works

This exercise is credit/no credit: 15 points or 0 points. Credit reflects a
real failure from your own project and an honest account of how you worked
through it. A failure you haven't fully fixed can earn credit if you say
what you tried and what you'd check next. Absent work or a made-up failure
receives no credit.
